# Skyward Airlines

A responsive fares and booking-enquiry website. The booking flow lets travellers choose Nairobi or a listed city as their origin, compare two indicative fare options, enter passenger details, request seat preferences, and send an enquiry through WhatsApp. Routes are limited to Nairobi and one listed city in either direction, using the published Nairobi fare per passenger and trip leg. Schedules, seat availability, and any child or infant pricing are confirmed by the Skyward team.
